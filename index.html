Hello! I'm a principal researcher in the Deep Learning Group at Microsoft Research.

  • I design programming languages for creating visual artifacts (Flint, PiCCL).
  • I build agentic tools that help people work with data (Data Formulator).
  • I do research on agent memory and simulators (PlugMem, StudentSim, ReUseIt).

I got my PhD in University of Washington. I was lucky to work with Ras Bodik and Alvin Cheung. I studied in Peking University and was lucky to work with Zhenjiang Hu and Yingfei Xiong.
